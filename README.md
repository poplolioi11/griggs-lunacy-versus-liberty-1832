# 《精神失常與自由》：肯辛頓院住院者的公開信（1832） / Lunacy versus Liberty — Transcription Dataset

William Griggs 於 1832 年出版這封致英國大法官的公開信，敘述自己被送入肯辛頓私人精神病院的經過、院內住宿與日常活動，以及獲准離院的過程。書中亦記載他所見其他住院者的處境，並以此檢討收治制度。

In this 1832 letter to the Lord Chancellor, William Griggs describes his admission to Kensington House in London, daily life and encounters with other patients there, and the circumstances of his release. He uses those experiences to argue for a review of the laws governing private asylums.

## Source and scope

University of Glasgow Library, digitised in partnership with Wellcome Collection, b21473109, Source frames 1–22. Original publication: London: Published by W. Griggs, 1832. Complete 1832 pamphlet. Seventeen image frames contain original print, including the title page and the author’s closing material. Five further frames identify unprinted surfaces or digitisation material.

## Files

- `transcription.txt` retains line and frame markers. Tabs mark table cells. 
- `transcription.json` identifies each frame and text or table unit. A null printed page means no printed page number was recorded. The source links identify the corresponding scan.
- `transcription.tei.xml` maps the same frame text and table cells into TEI P5.
- `review-notes.md` lists review coverage and reading decisions.
## Transcription and review

AI-assisted reading used fixed source scans. Published text preserves printed spellings and historical terminology. Spacing may be normalized. A square (□) indicates an unresolved character. See the notes for editorial omissions and uncertain readings.

Version 0.2.0. This dataset has no measured error rate or expert proofreading.
