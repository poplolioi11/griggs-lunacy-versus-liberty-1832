# Review notes

Version 0.1.0. Source frames 1–22.

Initial AI reading based on all 22 fixed whole scans. The 17 original printed text faces were checked directly against enlarged page images and compared with local Apple Vision results. Blank surfaces, markings and digitisation metadata are identified per frame. Layout hyphens inside a page were removed, genuine spellings retained, and an end-of-page split retained. Body font style and spacing before punctuation are simplified. The letter, postscript, verses, title matter and the visible signature remain individually navigable. A named correspondent and the explicitly published 1832 case accounts are scoped to their historical wording.
